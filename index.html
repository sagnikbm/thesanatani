The Raam Mandir Controversy

While the Uttar Pradesh Assembly election of 2027 is still months away, the political battle has already begun. For Yogi Adityanath and the BJP, the election will be about more than five years of governance. It will also be a referendum on the political and cultural promise that has defined the BJP's relationship with Uttar Pradesh: Ram Mandir and Ayodhya. When a major controversy emerges around an institution of such national and religious significance, public scrutiny inevitably reaches its most prominent political face. For Yogi Adityanath, as one of the central figures associated with the Ram Mandir project, this means mounting political pressure and opposition attacks, intensified media scrutiny, and growing demands for clear answers.
