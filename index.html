The Forgotten Temples of Rural India

Across rural India, ancient temples continue to serve as living centres of Sanatan tradition, community, memory and devotion.

Many of these temples are not simply monuments. They remain connected to local festivals, rituals, families and communities that have preserved their traditions across generations.

This article explores the forgotten temples of rural India and the people working to preserve their living heritage.
